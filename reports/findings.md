# Findings

_Generated by `src/report.py` from UCI Online Retail II (1,067,371 raw lines, Dec 2009 - 9 Dec 2011).
Currency: GBP. Report build time: 22 s from warehouse to finished Excel on the build machine._

## Data preparation that changes the numbers
| Issue | Lines | Effect if ignored |
|---|---:|---|
| Same invoices in both Excel sheets (1,088 invoices) | 22,523 | Dec 2010 double-counted |
| Exact duplicate lines | 11,812 | revenue overstated |
| Lines with no Customer ID | 235,151 | if dropped, **GBP 2,575,371 (13.1%) of gross sales disappears** |
| Fees, postage, manual & bad-debt lines | 5,823 | mixed into product revenue |
| Zero-price / negative-qty stock write-offs | 5,961 | distort units and price metrics |

## Headline
- Gross product sales GBP 19,643,847; cancellations GBP 716,426;
  **net sales GBP 18,927,421**.
- Strong Q4 seasonality: November 2011 was the peak month (net GBP 1,427,125, 2,751 orders).
- United Kingdom is 85% of net sales.

## Customers
- Revenue is concentrated: the top 1% of identified customers (59) bring
  31% of customer net sales; the top 20% bring 77%.
- Retention: 42% of the Dec-2009 group bought again 3 months later, but that group is the
  *existing* customer base (data starts that month). For genuinely new cohorts the 3-month figure is 21%.
  Treating the first month as "new customers" would overstate acquisition and retention.

## Cancellations - one order can move the month
- Medium Ceramic Top Storage Jar shows a 95% cancellation rate: almost all of it is one
  74,215-unit order entered and cancelled the same day (Jan 2011).
- December 2011 reports a 28.3% cancellation rate. Excluding same-day
  order-and-cancel pairs of 1,000+ units, it is **1.3%**. The monthly report should show both.
- December 2011 is also a **partial month** (data ends 9 Dec). Its -42% YoY change is an
  artefact; the Excel report flags it automatically.

## Process
- The whole monthly pack (KPI tables, regional split, charts, Excel workbook) regenerates from one command.
  The Excel report is formula-driven: choosing a month in one cell refreshes every KPI, MoM and YoY comparison,
  regional split and YTD figure, with a built-in check that regional totals tie to net sales.
